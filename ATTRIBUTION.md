# Attribution & Disclaimer

## Source data
Plateworks Ignition's calculations are derived from U.S. government works in the
public domain, published by the National Wildfire Coordinating Group (NWCG):

- **Incident Response Pocket Guide (IRPG), PMS 461** — Fine Fuel Moisture and
  Probability of Ignition tables (Table A, Tables B/C/D corrections, the
  Probability of Ignition table, and the Fine Fuel Moisture / Fire Behavior
  interpretation), January 2025 edition.
- **Temperature, Relative Humidity, and Dew Point Tables, PMS 437** — the
  psychrometric elevation-band reference used to validate the relative-humidity
  calculation.

Works produced by the U.S. federal government are not subject to copyright in
the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105).
